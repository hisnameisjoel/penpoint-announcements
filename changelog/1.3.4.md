Penpoint 1.3.4 brings comments and note pins right into your manuscript! Now you can finally keep track of the changes you want to make for your book right in Manuscript Mode.

::party-popper:: **New Features**

- **Comments in Your Manuscript**: Leave yourself notes in the page margin, right beside the line they belong to. Select some text and press Ctrl+Alt+M (or right-click and choose Add Comment or hit the Add Comment button in the toolbar), then write a note that shows up in a new sidebar. You can resolve notes when you're done (they're hidden until you reveal them) or delete it. A small floating button filters the margin to open or resolved comments, or hides them down to little dots. Comments are for you, not your readers: they never appear in your exports.
- **Pin Notes to Your Prose**: Right-click a selection and choose "Pin a Note" to keep a Notebook note in the margin beside a passage, with a live preview and a button to jump straight to the note. It works both ways: the note lists every place it's pinned, and clicking one takes you back to that exact spot in your manuscript.
- **Page Setup for Exports**: Exporting to Word, PDF, or RTF now lets you choose a paper size (Letter, A4, or 6" x 9" book trim) and adjust the margins and first line indent. The export preview shows your actual page shape and margins as you change them, and the three formats finally agree with each other on how your pages look.
- **Auto-Capitalize Sentences**: Turn it on in Settings and Penpoint capitalizes the first letter of every sentence as you type, so a long session never breaks stride on the Shift key. It knows that "Mr.", "e.g.", decimals, and ellipses aren't sentence breaks, and if it ever guesses wrong, Backspace right afterward puts your lowercase letter back. Off by default.

::feather:: **Improvements**

- **Longer Flowstate Sessions**: Write and edit stretches can now run up to 120 minutes instead of stopping at 99. The timer bar also stays on screen after you hit Stop, so you can adjust your times or start another session without summoning it back.
- **Empty Notes Clean Up After Themselves**: A note you make in the Minipen sidebar and leave without writing anything is now discarded instead of sitting in your notebook as an "Untitled." Your cursor starts in the title now so you can just start writing.
- **Removing a Tag From a Note**: Tags on a note in the Minipen sidebar now have a small X to take them back off, instead of being tucked inside the "add tags" button.
- **Sidebar Fits Your Screen Better**: The side navigation bar now makes better use of the vertical space it has, meaning less scrunching and better visibility on smaller screens.
- **Tag Controls Stay Put While You Scroll**: The buttons and bulk edit bar at the top of the Tags page now stay in place while your tag groups scroll underneath.

::bug:: **Bug Fixes**

- **Chapter Titles Are Now Real Headings**: Exported chapter titles now register as real headings. Word's navigation pane, tables of contents, and tools like Vellum and Atticus recognize every chapter, and e-readers show your real chapter titles instead of just "Chapter 2."
- **Indented Paragraphs in EPUB**: Paragraphs indented in the editor now read like normal book paragraphs on e-readers, instead of the whole paragraph being pushed sideways.
- **Image Captions and Controls**: Long captions now wrap onto a new line instead of getting cut off, and the picture toolbar no longer hides behind the formatting toolbar or runs off the edge of a narrow panel.
- **Pictures in the Minipen Sidebar**: Pictures in the sidebar now wrap your text and sit against the margin you chose, with smaller captions that read as labels rather than competing with your writing.
- **Import and Export Fixes**: Timeline events, note tags, and picture blocks now export (and note tags come back on import), heading levels and block quotes keep their shape, cleared fields no longer appear as empty headings, and the fill-in templates stopped over-tagging new characters.
- **Bold Inside a Heading**: Bolding a word inside a heading now makes it heavier instead of lighter, so bold reads as bold everywhere.
- **Commas After Linked Names**: A comma or period right after a character or place link no longer slides onto the next line by itself.

Thank you for using Penpoint! Happy writing.
