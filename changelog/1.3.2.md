Penpoint 1.3.2 is here, with a way to track a character's importance book by book, a new way to read your chapters, and a handful of fixes for Series and Corkboard users.

::party-popper:: **New Features**

- **Importance That Changes Book to Book**: A character who carries your first book can fade into the background by the third, and their Importance can now say so. Set it per book while viewing "This Book," and "All Books" shows you the whole picture at a glance. Items and locations work the same way, and standalone books are unchanged.
- **A Second Way to Read a Chapter: By Scene**: The Chapters page has always grouped a chapter's content by block, description, cast, and so on, all at once across the whole chapter. A new View Mode button flips you to By Scene instead, stacking each scene in turn with your layout repeated inside it. Penpoint remembers your choice, and By Group stays the default.
- **Placeholder Cards Inside Folders**: Placeholders can now live inside a chapter group, even one that holds no chapters yet, so you can rough out a whole section before a word of it is written. Promoting one now creates the chapter right inside that folder.
- **Corkboard Card Size**: The Corkboard's cog menu now lets you pick how big your index cards are. Compact fits more of your book on screen at once, Large gives you room to read your summaries, and Standard is what you've been using.

::feather:: **Improvements**

- **Merging Scenes Now Asks First**: Backspace at the start of a scene (or Delete at the end of the one above) used to join two scenes instantly with no warning. Penpoint now asks first, naming both scenes and what becomes of them.
- **Profile Pictures Show Up in More Places**: Character, item, and location profile pictures now show up in character sheet blocks, chapter cast and setting lists, item and location pages, the Minipen sidebar, and search results.
- **Colored Highlighting Everywhere**: The colored highlighter from Manuscript Mode now works in character, location, item, and chapter description fields too.
- **Chapter Notes on Your Index Cards**: The Corkboard's cards can now show your Chapter Notes, and you can write them right on the card, with bold and italic on their usual keyboard shortcuts.
- **Read a Note Without Leaving the Page**: Clicking a note linked to a Notebook Block now opens the note right inside the block with a Back button, instead of throwing you over to the Notebook.
- **Hide Empty Blocks**: A new cog menu option on the chapter page can tuck away any block you haven't filled in yet.
- **Clearing a Notebook Block**: A trash icon now clears a Notebook Block for that character or scene, and picking a new one takes one click instead of two.
- **Longer Location Names**: Location names can now be up to 45 characters, matching your characters.

::bug:: **Bug Fixes**

- **Folders and Placeholders**: Empty chapter folders now land, and stay, exactly where you drop them. Promoting a placeholder that sat inside a folder now keeps the new chapter in that folder, and placeholder cards no longer vanish when a book is copied into a Series.
- **Windows ARM Updates**: Windows ARM builds are back for good after being dropped a couple of releases back.
- **Clickable Names and Search**: Character, item, and location names in the Chapters view are clickable again, clearing a chapter's subtitle or summary now sticks, and search no longer surfaces characters that only matched on internal bookkeeping.
- **Location Nesting and Series Import**: Sub-locations now show at the right nesting level, dragging one somewhere it can't go shows you why, and location types no longer duplicate when copying a book into a Series.
- **Corkboard Texture**: The Corkboard's paper grain now covers the whole board evenly on books with lots of chapters.

Thank you for using Penpoint! Happy writing.
