Penpoint v1.2.1 is here! This release adds two big expansions to the scene and calendar systems, a handful of smart workflow improvements, and a round of bug fixes including one that could silently clear scene descriptions.

::party-popper:: **New Features**

- **Pick Chapters by the Scene**: When adding a character, item, location, or plot point to a chapter, you can now see and pick exactly which scenes to assign. Chapters with multiple scenes expand in the picker so you can tick the whole chapter or individual scenes. Already-assigned entries are hidden. The Manuscript Mode sidebars also now list the specific scenes you've tagged under each chapter.
- **Eras for Custom Calendars**: Custom calendars now support a full era system. In Calendar Settings, choose how dates display: plain years, a BCE/CE-style split with your own before/after labels, or named eras with their own start dates like "Reign of King Valdris." Pick from four date phrasings and your dates use that style throughout the app. Gaps between eras fall back to a Base era automatically.
- **Copy Blocks Across Scenes**: Right-click any block in a multi-scene chapter and copy it to all other scenes at once. Or choose "Copy all blocks to all scenes" to sync your entire scene's data everywhere at once. A confirmation dialog shows which scene you're copying from before anything changes.
- **Penpoint Startup Safety Net**: In the rare event Penpoint can't start, you'll see a clear screen confirming your books are safe, with buttons to restart, email support, or save a log file to attach to your message.

::feather:: **Improvements**

- **Arc Plot Point Rework**: Adding a plot point is now one searchable dropdown instead of two clicks. Type "climax" and every arc's climax shows up in a single list. Tick more than one at a time. On the Chapters page, what used to be two competing Story Arcs sections is now one block with clear "Whole chapter" and per-scene sub-sections.
- **Chapter-Wide Plot Points**: Restored the ability to assign plot points to an entire chapter separately from individual scenes. Removing a chapter-level plot point no longer touches scene-level ones.
- **Cleaner Detail Pages**: On Location pages, "Characters & Items" is now split into separate Characters and Items sections, each with its own search. The Add buttons on Characters, Items, Location, Chapters, and Arcs pages got a quieter look: a dashed row at the bottom of each list instead of obnoxious buttons.

::bug:: **Bug Fixes**

- **Scene Descriptions and Notes Could Disappear**: On rare occasions, the Description and Notes blocks on chapter scenes could go empty when interacting with arc assignments. Fixed at the source, with added safeguards. If you were affected, your text is safe in your automatic backups. Message us on Discord and we'll walk you through restoring it.
- **Undo for Accidental Scene Merges**: Accidentally merged two scenes with Backspace? Ctrl+Z (Cmd+Z on Mac) now brings the absorbed scene back fully intact: title, color, status, custom fields, and assignments. Works for right-click merges too.
- **Deleting a Scene Now Refreshes Instantly**: Deleted scenes no longer linger in the Manuscript navigator and editor. They disappear right away everywhere, as they should.
- **Export From the Book Library**: Right-clicking a book and choosing "Export" wasn't working. Now it opens a save dialog so you can save your book as a .penpoint backup file wherever you like.
- **Enter Key in Chapter Picker**: Hitting Enter on a highlighted chapter or scene now adds it and closes the popup, as expected. Previously it required a separate tick first.
- **Editing Book Cover Could Lock the Save Button**: If you uploaded a cover image for the first time without changing anything else, the Save Changes button would stay grayed out. Fixed.
- **Backup Imports Could Show Outdated Book Settings**: Reimporting a .penpoint backup could occasionally restore an older book cover. Your most recent covers now always travel with the backup.

Thank you for using Penpoint! Happy writing.
