Penpoint 1.4.0 is FINALLY here, and Penpoint Cloud comes with it! Your books can now back themselves up automatically, follow you across up to five computers, be available on the upcoming Minipen mobile app, and it's all fully end-to-end encrypted so only you can read them. Penpoint itself still works completely offline and stays yours forever. Alongside Penpoint Cloud there's a brand new Scrivener import and export, Note Templates (Notes can now travel between books in your library), draft comparison, a built-in way to contact support, and a VERY long list of improvements and fixes.

::party-popper:: **New Features**

- **Penpoint Cloud**: Back up every book automatically, then restore your whole library on another computer when you need it. Your books are encrypted on your computer before they leave it, and a 12-word Recovery Kit helps you regain access if you forget your Cloud password.
- **Penpoint Sync**: Keep up to five computers current, including your place in each book and your settings. Changes made on two computers merge on their own, and if the same scene changes on both, each version is kept as a Scene Draft you can compare side by side.
- **Set Up a New Computer Faster**: Sign in from the welcome screen and your books, theme, and settings arrive without repeating setup.
- **Restore From Cloud**: Restore any book missing from this computer, including one you recently deleted, and check stored copies from Settings. Restoring a backup over a Cloud book makes that version the book on every computer.
- **Manage Your Cloud Account and Subscription**: See your signed-in computers, manage account details, switch plans, cancel, or resume from Settings with clear pricing before you confirm, pause Cloud on one computer, or close Cloud without touching the books on your computer. When you change your Cloud password, you can tick a box to sign your other computers out. Nothing on them is deleted; they just need an emailed code and your new password to get back in.
- **Scrivener Import and Export**: Bring a Scrivener 3 project into Penpoint as a new book, with your chapters, scenes, notes, comments, and snapshots, and your character and place sheets become characters and locations. Going the other way, export your manuscript, or your whole book with its worldbuilding, as a Scrivener 3 project, saved scene versions included. Penpoint never changes or overwrites a Scrivener project you already have.
- **Note Templates**: Save any note as a reusable template from its toolbar or right-click menu, or start a blank one in the Templates folder. Create new notes from them in any book or series, and with Penpoint Cloud your templates follow you to your other computers.
- **Compare Scene and Chapter Drafts**: Compare a saved draft with your current writing to see added, removed, moved, and missing passages. Arrows jump from one change to the next.
- **Contact Support**: The Help menu (the ? icon) now has Contact Support for reporting a bug, sharing an idea, or asking a question without leaving Penpoint. It can attach a screenshot of your page, up to four images, and diagnostic logs, and "See what's included" shows you everything before you press Send. If a page crashes, the error screen has a Send to Penpoint button that fills in the details for you.
- **Standard Submission Format**: Pick it from the export formats and Penpoint writes the Word manuscript literary agents expect: Times New Roman, double spaced, indented paragraphs, a centered # between scenes, and your surname, title, and page number at the top of each page. The title page carries your contact details and a rounded word count, and it keeps its own settings so your everyday Word exports stay just as you like them.
- **Book Language**: Each book (or a whole series) can have its own spellcheck language, quote style, and autocorrect settings, so writing in two languages no longer means changing settings every time you switch books. Find it at the top of Settings, Spellcheck & Autocorrect. Japanese, Chinese, and Korean books also get matching fonts.
- **Scenes Come In With Your Manuscript**: Importing a manuscript from Word, Scrivener, Dabble, or another app now splits it into separate scenes wherever you marked a break with #, * * *, or a divider line. The preview shows how many scenes each chapter gets, and you can untick Split scenes at scene breaks to keep one scene per chapter.
- **Import RTF Files**: Open .rtf manuscripts alongside .docx, .epub, and plain-text files, with rich-text formatting and chapter breaks intact.
- **Justify Text**: Manuscript Mode's toolbar now has a Justify button next to Left, Center, and Right. Justified paragraphs carry through to Word, EPUB, and Rich Text exports; PDF and Standard Submission Format stay left-aligned.
- **Right-Click Menu for Text Fields**: Cut, Copy, Paste, and Select All now open from a right-click menu on every text box in Penpoint, from chapter titles and character names to nicknames, arc descriptions, and the Quick Create forms.
- **Backup Protection During Setup**: Choose a secondary backup folder during setup, including a cloud drive or another safe location.
- **Add Chapters Straight Into a Folder**: Click a folder in the chapter outline, then use + New Chapter, and the new chapter lands at the end of that folder. You can also right-click a folder and choose New Chapter in Group.
- **More in the Minipen**: Minipen can follow the chapter you are writing (off by default), the Arcs tab has a Series Arc pill for series, and Characters, Items, and Locations have a Delete button at the bottom of the page in edit mode.
- **New Single-Quote Style**: Smart quotes' Single quotes menu now offers ‚ ’ (Low-high · 9-close), the partner to the „ ” double-quote style used in Polish, Dutch, and other languages.

::feather:: **Improvements**

- **Tables Beside Your Manuscript**: In the Minipen, a table now shows as a card you can read right next to your writing. The first rows are visible, wide tables scroll sideways, and a small arrow folds it away. Edit opens the full table.
- **More Control Over Exported Pages**: Word and Rich Text exports can use Times New Roman, Georgia, or Courier New with single, 1.5, or double spacing, and Word files can carry a running header. Title pages add a contact block, an optional word count, separate pen name and legal name fields, and a choice of whether your subtitle prints.
- **Export Settings Follow You**: Your name and contact details carry across every export format, and with Penpoint Cloud your export choices reach your other computers too.
- **Series Books Export Under Their Own Names**: Exporting a Book from your Series titles it with that Book's name and adds a "Book 2 of" line for your Series underneath, no retyping needed.
- **Chapter Numbers Without Titles**: You can turn off chapter titles in an export and keep just "Chapter 1", so chapters you titled by number no longer print it twice.
- **New Chapters Land Where You Want**: Adding a chapter from the Chapters page now puts it right after the chapter you have selected, inside its folder too. Splitting a chapter lets you add a subtitle and turn numbering off, and shows which chapter number the new one will get.
- **Sort by Importance Starts With Your Main Cast**: Choosing Importance in the Characters and Items sort menus now puts Primary first, and the order options read "Most important first" and "Least important first" instead of Ascending and Descending.
- **Smarter Manuscript Imports**: The picker now shows .doc, .pages, .odt, and .pdf files and tells you how to convert them into something Penpoint can import, and a short centered line under a chapter title, like a point-of-view character's name, comes in as that chapter's subtitle.
- **Exporting a Book**: Export Book explains the .penpoint file, then shows where it saved and offers to open its folder.
- **Era Change Heads-Up**: Changing or deleting an era in Calendar Settings now shows which of your existing dates will look different, so you can undo it before it takes effect. Rather skip it? Tick "Don't show this again."
- **Character Page Keeps Its Header**: The Edit button and character name stay in reach while you scroll.
- **Tag Creation, Unified**: Creating a tag from Ctrl+N now matches Tag Settings, including choosing or creating its group without leaving the tag.
- **Faster Mode Switching**: Switching between Worldbuilding and Manuscript mode is quicker now.
- **Cleaner Saved Logs**: When you save logs from Settings, Advanced, the saved copy has your computer's user name, folder paths, email addresses, and sign-in tokens removed, so it is safer to share. The logs on your computer are not changed.
- **Windows Startup Help**: A clear repair guide helps when Microsoft WebView2 prevents Penpoint from opening.
- **Primary Storage Location Moves**: Moving your Primary Storage Location is more reliable, so you get back to your library more smoothly.

::bug:: **Bug Fixes**

- **Comments and Pinned Notes on Mac**: Cmd+Option+M now adds a comment, and Ctrl+clicking selected text no longer loses your selection, so Add Comment and Pin a Note work from the right-click menu again.
- **Suggestions Land Where They Should**: On macOS, accepting a ProWritingAid or Grammarly suggestion no longer lands one character off below headings, lists, and quotes.
- **Importing Manuscripts**: Penpoint now spots chapter titles with spelled-out numbers ("Chapter Two"), keeps your Part pages and numbering intact, and leaves a typed table of contents out of your chapter list. If an import fails partway, it removes what it had already added and explains in plain English what went wrong, so trying again never leaves duplicate chapters or an empty book behind.
- **Importing Markdown Manuscripts**: A line of hash marks (like # or # # #) now becomes a scene break, and a Scrivener Markdown export keeps every paragraph separate, drops the stray # from chapter titles, and no longer turns its title and author header into an extra chapter.
- **Numbered Lists and EPUB Re-Import**: A numbered list that starts above 1, or picks up again after text, keeps its numbers in the editor and every export, and EPUB files exported from Penpoint no longer come back as empty chapters when imported again.
- **Exports Open and Print Cleanly**: Text pasted in from Word no longer leaves hidden characters that could stop an exported file from opening, Word files no longer open with every line centered in Apple Pages, chapter numbers, subtitles, and contact details print in black instead of faint grey, and a failed export no longer leaves a broken .penpoint file behind.
- **Series Exports**: Exporting a Series now numbers chapters the way Penpoint shows them, gives each Book its own folder, and labels chapters from other Books with their Book and chapter number (like "Book Two, Chapter 3: Natalie").
- **Window Opening**: On a Mac, Penpoint's window no longer flashes and jumps around as it opens, and on Linux it now remembers your window size and position.
- **Creating and Editing Books**: If Penpoint can't create, save, or duplicate a book (or add one to a series), the window now stays open and tells you why.
- **Empty Folders and Index Cards Stay Put**: Empty chapter folders no longer slide up or down a spot in the outline, and deleting or merging away a chapter no longer sends the index cards after it to the top.
- **Outline Blocks**: Backspace at the start of a bullet now joins it to the line above, an empty bullet can be deleted with Backspace, and Enter at the end of a bullet with sub-bullets no longer moves them under the new blank bullet.
- **Spellcheck and Dictionary**: Spellcheck no longer underlines whole phrases of Japanese, Chinese, or Thai text, and adding "sunsteel" after "Sunsteel" now works instead of silently doing nothing.
- **Calendar Blocks**: Typing a year no longer sometimes saves a different year when your calendar uses named eras, and a carried-over last-used date is kept when you leave edit mode.
- **Series Names**: Converting a book into a series, or renaming a series, now warns you when another book or series already has that name, and split-off books are numbered (like "Dune #2") instead of sitting beside an identical title.
- **Damaged WebP Pictures**: Adding a damaged WebP picture as a portrait, cover, or Notebook image can no longer close Penpoint on a computer with little memory.
- **And a Pile of Smaller Fixes**: Comment margins, story arc labels, the Add item list, Minipen picture clicks, toolbar menus that scrolled your manuscript, dropdowns running off screen, arrow keys jumping between entries, and more are sorted. The full list is under See All Versions in Settings, About.

Thank you for using Penpoint! Happy writing.
