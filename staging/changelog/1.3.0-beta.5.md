This is staging beta 5 of v1.3.0, building on beta 4 with a new font, smarter Series merging, and a more series-aware Timeline.

::party-popper:: **New Features**

- **EB Garamond Font**: A new font option, a classic serif with an old-style, literary feel. Pick it from Settings under Interface, where the font list now previews every option in its own typeface. Carries over cleanly to your PDF, Word, and EPUB exports too.

::feather:: **Improvements**

- **Smarter Series Merge Conflict Reporting**: Merging a book into a Series now shows you exactly which fields conflicted (like Name, Importance, Type, or Number blocks) instead of silently dropping them, so you can resolve them yourself.
- **Timeline Gets Series-Aware**: In a Series book, the Timeline's Focus toggle now scopes to the active book, chapter columns follow your book's own chapter order, and color-coded bands mark which chapters belong to which book. You can also grab and pan across the timeline.
- **Library Help Menu Fixed**: The "Books Guide" help option on the Library screen now actually opens the guide instead of doing nothing.

Thank you for testing Penpoint staging! Report any issues before production release.
