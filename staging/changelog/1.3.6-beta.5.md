Staging beta 5 of v1.3.6 is a fuller real-world test of Penpoint Cloud. Please try setting up a new or returning computer, bringing back a whole library, then saving, backing up, and reopening it. Please also try a Series with chapter groups.

::party-popper:: **New Features**

- **Penpoint Cloud**: Your books can back up automatically and stay available from another computer, while Penpoint itself remains yours and works offline.
- **Faster Cloud Signin**: When loading Penpoint onto a new computer, choose "Already have Penpoint Cloud?" during the first screen to sign in and bring back your books, theme, and settings without repeating the Onboarding welcome questions.
- **Restore From Cloud**: Bring back a book that is missing from this computer, including a recently deleted one, and use Check Your Backups to open its stored copy.
- **Onboarding Secondary Backup Location**: During the Onboarding experience, Penpoint will ask you to choose a secondary backup folder after asking for your interface preferences, so your books are safer from the very beginning.
- **Compare Saved Drafts V2**: Open a saved Scene or Chapter Draft beside the current version to see what changed, including writing that moved or was removed.

::feather:: **Improvements**

- **Your Library Returns Together**: A new or returning computer can bring several books into place at once by downloading multiple books at the same time.
- **Better Writing Across Computers**: The whole Book Library synchronization workflow has been vastly improved, offering far less "stuck books" and Manual Backups needed, and far better reconciling of merging conflicts across Books and Series.
- **Better Primary Backup Location System**: Moving your Primary Storage Location is more reliable, and moving the primary backup location has been put under the "Danger Zone" to prevent accidental adjustments.
- **Your Cloud Account**: Manage signed-in devices, pause Cloud on this computer, and change, cancel, or resume your subscription from Penpoint.

::bug:: **Bug Fixes**

- **Recovering After a Cloud Mismatch**: If a computer needs to reconcile with Cloud, Penpoint now keeps a safety backup, preserves conflicting writing as Scene Drafts, and lets you retry right away.
- **Keeping Your Newest Writing**: Fixed timing problems that could let an older version of an Item, Location, or Relationship win over your newest edit.
- **Returning to Cloud**: Older sign-ins and interrupted device cleanup now recover more cleanly, with clearer paths back into your account.
- **Backups From a Newer Penpoint**: Penpoint now asks you to update when a backup needs a newer app version, instead of showing a confusing error.
- **Staging Book Copies**: Copies saved in Penpoint Staging now show the version warning instead of becoming unable to open after replacement.

Thank you for testing Penpoint staging! Report any issues before production release.
