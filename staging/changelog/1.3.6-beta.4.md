This is staging beta 4 of v1.3.6. This round is mostly about Sync staying quiet and honest: a rare stuck-sync case now sorts itself out, opening a book after edits elsewhere is faster, and Cloud status messages stop stacking confusing notices. A few billing and export rough edges got smoothed out too.

::feather:: **Improvements**

- **Books Open on Two Computers Stay Current**: An idle open book now quietly catches up with edits made on another computer, without needing to close and reopen it, and your typing is never interrupted while it happens.
- **A Clearer Wait When Opening a Book**: If opening a book takes a moment because another computer is still syncing, Penpoint now says so instead of leaving you waiting with no explanation.
- **Exporting a Book**: Export Book now explains what a .penpoint file is before it saves one, then shows you where the file landed with a button to open its folder, instead of a note that vanished after ten seconds.
- **See What Kind of Device Each One Is**: Every computer and phone signed in to Penpoint Cloud now carries its own mark in Manage Devices, so you can tell a laptop from a phone at a glance. Each one fills its mark in the next time it connects.

::bug:: **Bug Fixes**

- **A Book That Could Get Stuck After Edits on Two Computers**: Fixed a rare case where a book edited on two computers at once could stop syncing instead of sorting itself out on its own.
- **Recently Deleted Books Keep Their Names**: In Restore from Cloud, previewing a deleted book without recovering it no longer replaces its name with "Name not available." The list also shows a history icon instead of a trash can, so it reads as a record of what was deleted rather than a delete button.
- **Faster Opening After Edits on Another Computer**: When a book has to merge changes from another computer as it opens, the cloud copy is now double-checked in the background afterward instead of while you wait. Large books on a slow connection could sit on the opening screen for a minute or more.
- **Switching to Yearly Billing**: Penpoint now asks you to confirm the exact amount before charging your card, tells you plainly if your bank has not approved the payment yet instead of celebrating early, no longer cuts off days you already paid for when a payment fails, and adds a Billing history link next to Manage billing.
- **Clearer Cloud Status Messages**: Backup health no longer stacks duplicate or misleading notices, and only offers a Sync button when it can actually do something.
- **Preferences Overwritten by an Older Computer**: Fixed a rare case where a computer running an older version of Penpoint could overwrite your preferences from a newer one.
- **Reading Backups From a Newer Version**: Penpoint now recognizes when a backup was saved by a newer version of the app and asks you to update instead of showing a confusing error.
- **A Half-Written File Left Behind by a Failed Export**: A book export that fails partway no longer leaves a broken .penpoint file sitting where you chose to save it.
- **The Manage Devices List Flickering**: The device list no longer blinks back to "Loading" while you're reading it, and opens instantly the second time.
- **Splitting a Series Into Books**: Fixed the confirmation window sometimes hiding its own button on certain screen sizes, and gave it more breathing room.
- **Bringing Back a Subtrait You Removed**: Typing the name of a removed subtrait now restores it instead of quietly refusing, and renaming a trait or subtrait to a name that's already taken now tells you so on the spot.

Thank you for testing Penpoint staging! Report any issues before production release.
