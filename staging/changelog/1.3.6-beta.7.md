Staging beta 7 of v1.3.6 is the "Cloud Ready" build: the last big round before Penpoint Cloud opens to everyone. It brings Scrivener import, a per-book writing language, and a much calmer Cloud when things go sideways. Please try importing a Scrivener project, setting a book to another language, and comparing a scene that changed on two computers.

::party-popper:: **New Features**

- **Scrivener Import and Export**: Bring a Scrivener 3 project into Penpoint as a new book, with chapters, scenes, notes, comments, and snapshots, and your character and place sheets as characters and locations. Export your manuscript or your whole book, worldbuilding included, back to Scrivener anytime.
- **Book Language**: Give each book (or a whole series) its own spellcheck language, quote style, and autocorrect settings in Settings under Book Settings. Japanese, Chinese, and Korean also get matching fonts.
- **Add Chapters Straight Into a Folder**: Click a folder in the chapter outline, then + New Chapter, and the chapter lands at the end of that folder. Right-click a folder for New Chapter in Group.
- **New Single-Quote Style**: Smart quotes now offer a Low-high single-quote style with a 9-shaped close, the partner to the low-high double quotes used in Polish, Dutch, and other languages.

::feather:: **Improvements**

- **Clearer Two-Device Reviews**: When a scene changes on two devices, Penpoint flags it in your outline and opens both versions side by side, showing which device each came from and which is newer. Step through every change and keep your current writing with one click.
- **Cleaner, Sharper Draft Comparisons**: The compare screen is tidier, with arrows to jump between changes, and long scenes now highlight only the passages that changed.
- **Calm Messages During Cloud Maintenance**: Restoring, browsing saved versions, deleting from Cloud, and Verify backups now say so in one calm amber card, and waiting books pick back up on their own when maintenance ends.
- **Opening a Book That Hasn't Caught Up**: If Penpoint can't fetch the latest Cloud version when you open a book, it catches the book up on its own before treating that computer as the safe place to edit.
- **Penpoint Says When It Is Too Old to Sync**: When Cloud needs a newer Penpoint, the Save menu, your library, and Backup health now say "Penpoint needs updating to sync".
- **Creating and Editing Books**: If Penpoint can't create, save, or duplicate a book, the window stays open and tells you why.
- **Importing Manuscripts**: Spelled-out chapter numbers ("Chapter Two") are recognized, Part pages and numbering stay intact, failed imports clean up after themselves and explain what went wrong, and short centered lines under a chapter title become its subtitle.
- **Import File Picker**: The picker now shows .doc, .pages, .odt, and .pdf files and tells you how to convert them.
- **Roomier Paragraph Spacing on Mac**: Paragraph spacing on macOS is a little roomier at every setting, which keeps writing assistant suggestions accurate.

::bug:: **Bug Fixes**

- **Deleted Books Stay Deleted**: A book you move into a series, a series you split, or an empty book left by a failed import no longer comes back on your other computers.
- **No More Surprise Reloads**: Your book no longer closes and reopens while you type, and no longer saves a "kept from your other device" copy when you only wrote on one computer.
- **No More Empty Versions**: An empty copy of a scene from another device no longer asks you to choose, and an empty deleted scene no longer reappears.
- **Suggestions Land Where They Should**: On macOS, ProWritingAid and Grammarly suggestions no longer land one character off below chapter headings, lists, and quotes.
- **Empty Folders Stay Put**: Empty chapter folders no longer slide around after you add, delete, split, or merge chapters, or after Cloud syncs another device's changes.
- **Comments and Pinned Notes on Mac**: Cmd+Option+M adds a comment, and Ctrl+clicking selected text keeps your selection.
- **Numbered Lists and Scene Breaks in Imports**: Numbered lists keep their numbers, and a line of # marks in Markdown becomes a scene break.
- **Stuck Picture Backups Repair Themselves**: A picture whose Cloud copy didn't match now repairs itself from your good copy.
- **Note Templates Return After a Cloud Reset**: Penpoint backs them up again on the next sync.
- **Still Syncing Clears After Maintenance**: An open book no longer keeps saying "Still syncing" after maintenance ends.
- **Switching Books in a Series**: Clicking a different book in a series from the Library opens the right one.
- **Writing Stats Catch Up After Upgrading**: Stuck daily writing stats from an earlier beta fold back in on the next start, without counting anything twice.
- **Spellcheck and Japanese Text**: Spellcheck no longer underlines whole phrases of Japanese, Chinese, or Thai text.
- **Demo Book Cloud Status**: The Sherlock Holmes demo book no longer says your account is locked.

Thank you for testing Penpoint staging! Report any issues before production release.
