This is staging beta 3 of v1.3.6. This round adds a way to pause Cloud on one computer, a new draft comparison view, and a batch of fixes for the multi-computer recovery cases that came up in beta 1 and 2. If you hit a stuck sync or a book that would not reconnect after cancelling and rejoining, please give it another try.

::party-popper:: **New Features**

- **Pause and Resume Penpoint Cloud**: Pause Cloud backup on this computer from Penpoint Cloud settings and resume it whenever you are ready.
- **Compare Scene and Chapter Drafts**: Open any saved draft beside the current version to see exactly what writing was removed or added, including scenes that moved, appeared, or disappeared.
- **Minipen Can Follow Your Writing**: Turn on "Link to the active chapter" from a chapter's gear icon and Minipen's Chapters section opens each chapter's notes as you move through your manuscript. Off by default.

::bug:: **Bug Fixes**

- **Cloud Recovery After Cancelling and Rejoining**: Computers whose local sync history no longer matches Cloud now take a safety backup, reconcile both copies automatically, preserve conflicting writing as Scene Drafts, and can retry immediately with Sync now instead of staying stuck.
- **Cloud Staying Unlocked on Linux**: Linux computers that can safely store Cloud keys now remember their unlock between launches, and turning off password-at-launch takes effect immediately.
- **A Manuscript Conflict Between Two Computers Going Unnoticed**: When the same scene was edited on two of your computers, Penpoint saved both versions but the second copy was easy to miss. It now tells you right inside the book the moment you open it, with one tap to jump straight to the saved copy.
- **A Stuck "Two Versions" Notice**: The "some scenes have two versions" note now clears once you have viewed the saved copy, and its button takes you straight to that copy instead of a backup button that could not clear it.
- **Scene Merges Updating Right Away**: Merging scenes now shows the combined writing immediately, without needing to refresh Penpoint.
- **A Setup Screen When Switching Books**: Switching between books quickly could briefly show a "Database Setup Required" screen instead of opening your book. Penpoint now waits out the momentary delay and takes you straight in.
- **Character Page Keeps Its Header**: Scroll deep into a character and a slim bar stays pinned at the top, keeping the Edit button in reach and sliding in the character's name so you always know whose page you are on.
- **Keeping Your Newest Edits When Syncing**: Fixed a behind-the-scenes timing mismatch that could let Sync keep an older version of an Item, Location, or Relationship instead of your most recent edit.
- **Missing Cloud Badge on Series Books**: Drilling into a series used to hide its cloud badge, so a synced series could look unlinked. Every Book inside a series now shows the same cloud status as the series itself.
- **Unnamed Computers in Manage Devices**: Manage Devices now shows each computer's name instead of "Unnamed device," including computers already affected. Any custom name stays exactly as you set it.
- **Dropdown Menus Cut Off at the Top**: Long dropdown lists, like picking a location type, could run off the top of the window with no way to scroll. They now stay on screen and scroll neatly with a scrollbar.

Thank you for testing Penpoint staging! Report any issues before production release.
