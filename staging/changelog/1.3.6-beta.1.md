This is staging beta 1 of v1.3.6, and it is the first build with Penpoint Cloud inside it: automatic encrypted backup, sync across your computers, and a Recovery Kit that means only you can ever open your books. You will find it under Settings, in the Penpoint Cloud (Beta) section. This is a test playground: keep your real writing in your normal Penpoint, use a throwaway book here, and know that everything in the beta cloud is cleared when the beta ends.

::party-popper:: **New Features**

- **Penpoint Cloud**: Set it up once from Settings and your books start backing themselves up, every book and every version, with nothing to do by hand. A small cloud badge on each book on your shelf shows when it is safely stored.
- **Your Books Outlive Your Computer**: Lost laptop, dead drive, or a brand new machine: sign in on another computer and your library comes back exactly where you left it. Use Restore from Cloud on the library shelf to bring back any book that is not on this computer, and Check your backups in Settings to open each one and prove it really saved.
- **Penpoint Sync**: Keeps up to five computers in step automatically. Edit a chapter on one, open it on the other, and the change is there. It even carries your sense of place: the same book, the same mode, the same page, and your cursor back where you stopped.
- **Only You Can Read It**: Everything is encrypted on your computer before it leaves, protected by a password you choose. Penpoint stores your books but cannot open them, and neither can anyone else. Your 12-word Recovery Kit is your way back in if you ever forget that password, and you see it exactly once, so save it somewhere safe.
- **Manage Your Devices**: See every computer signed in to your account, hand the active device from one to another, and remove one you no longer use.
- **Close Your Account Cleanly**: A Danger zone at the bottom of the Cloud settings lets you close your account entirely. It only ever touches the cloud copies; the books on your computer stay exactly as they are.

::feather:: **Beta Notes**

- **Fake Money Only**: Choosing a plan opens a checkout in your browser. Use the test card 4242 4242 4242 4242 with any future expiry, any 3-digit code, and any real-looking address. No real money is ever charged.
- **Your Sign-In Code**: Codes arrive by email and can take a minute. Check your spam or junk folder, and do not worry if the sender looks unfamiliar.
- **A Separate App**: The beta installs alongside your normal Penpoint with its own icon and its own books. It cannot see or touch your real library. Windows may warn that it does not recognize the app; click More info, then Run anyway.
- **What To Tell Me**: The signup flow, anything confusing or broken (a screenshot is gold), and any screen that says your writing was deleted or lost when you did not ask for it. That last one is a bug, every time.
- **Everything Up To v1.3.5 Is Here Too**: Chapter Drafts, the Authors Dictionary, margin comments, pinned notes, page setup for exports, and every fix since v1.3.0.

Thank you for testing Penpoint staging! Report any issues before production release.
