Staging beta 6 of v1.3.6 is our "one good pass" on Penpoint Cloud, plus a big round of export upgrades and the new Note Templates. Please try saving a note as a template and using it in another book, exporting in Standard Submission Format, and working in one book across two computers.

::party-popper:: **New Features**

- **Note Templates**: Save any note as a reusable template from its toolbar or right-click menu, or start a blank one in the Templates folder. Use your templates in any book or series, and with Penpoint Cloud they follow you to your other computers.
- **Standard Submission Format**: Pick it from the export formats and Penpoint writes the Word document literary agents expect: Times New Roman, double spaced, a centered # between scenes, and your surname, title, and page number at the top of each page.
- **Export to Scrivener**: Export your manuscript as a Scrivener 3 project, with chapters as folders, scenes as documents, and each scene's description on its index card.
- **Right-Click Menu for Text Fields**: Chapter titles, character names, and other simple text fields now have Cut, Copy, Paste, and Select All on right-click.
- **Sign Out Everywhere Else**: When you change your Cloud password, you can tick a box to sign your other computers out. Nothing on them is deleted.

::feather:: **Improvements**

- **Cloud Never Gets Stuck Waiting**: Penpoint no longer re-uploads pictures the cloud already has, and any sync pause now retries on its own within five minutes.
- **More Control Over Exported Pages**: Choose Times New Roman, Georgia, or Courier New with single, 1.5, or double spacing, add a running header in Word, and fill in a contact block, word count, and pen name on the title page.
- **Export Settings Follow You**: Your name and contact details carry across every export format, and with Penpoint Cloud your export choices reach your other computers too.
- **Series Books Export Under Their Own Names**: Exporting a Book from a Series uses that Book's title, with a "Book 2 of" line underneath.
- **Chapter Numbers Without Titles**: Turn off chapter titles in an export and keep just "Chapter 1".
- **Sort by Importance Starts With Your Main Cast**: Importance sorting now puts Primary characters and items first, with clearer order names.
- **Signing Back Into Cloud**: Signing back in with an emailed code now asks for your Cloud password too.
- **Clearer Wrong Password Message**: If Cloud turns down your password, Penpoint reminds you that you may have changed it on another computer.

::bug:: **Bug Fixes**

- **Cloud Takeover Screen**: The takeover screen no longer leaves part of your book visible at larger display or font sizes.
- **Comment Margins**: Chapter text no longer stretches edge to edge when a chapter has a comment.
- **Add Item Picker**: The "Add item" list now shows each item's real icon and archetype name.
- **Item and Location Pictures in Minipen**: Clicking one now opens it full size, just like character pictures.
- **Exports Print Cleanly**: Chapter numbers, subtitles, and contact details now print in black instead of faint grey.
- **Pasted Text Breaking Exports**: Text pasted from Word or another app no longer leaves hidden characters that could stop an export from opening.
- **Word Exports in Pages**: Exported Word files no longer open with every line centered in Apple Pages.

Thank you for testing Penpoint staging! Report any issues before production release.
