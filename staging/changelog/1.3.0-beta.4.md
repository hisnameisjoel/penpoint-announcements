This is staging beta 4 of v1.3.0, a big one. Series, a rebuilt import wizard, and Events lead the way, alongside a long list of improvements and fixes.

::party-popper:: **New Features**

- **Introducing Series**: Your trilogy, saga, or seven-book epic can now live together as one Series with a shared cast and world. Turn a book you've already written into Book 1 (nothing is lost), then add as many new or existing books as your story needs. Characters, locations, and items are shared across every book, so you write Sherlock once and he stays current everywhere. A new Series Arc view charts the threads that run through the whole saga, and your series shows up as a single stacked card in your library.
- **Import Locations, Notes, and Everything Else**: The import wizard now handles all six kinds of worldbuilding: characters, locations, items, notes, chapters, and arcs. Exports round-trip cleanly too, every field, trait, image, and @mention lands back exactly where it was. Note: files exported by older Penpoint versions use the old format, so re-export from this version first.
- **Introducing Events**: The big moments in your story finally have a home. Create an Event like "The Duel at Dawn," link it to the characters, items, and locations involved, and it shows up on every one of their pages, shared not copied. Events keep a single shared order across your book and lay the groundwork for the upcoming Timeline.
- **Open .penpoint Books by Double-Clicking**: Your computer now recognizes .penpoint files, so double-clicking one opens it straight into your library. Great for sharing a book with a friend or syncing through a cloud drive.
- **Highlight in Any Color**: Manuscript Mode's highlighter now offers a full palette or a custom color, not just yellow. Right-click text to add or remove a highlight on the spot.
- **Quote Marks for Every Language**: Smart Quotes now speaks more than English. Choose how your quote marks curl: English, Finnish and Swedish, German, Polish, or French guillemets, from Settings under Autocorrect.
- **OpenDyslexic Accessibility Font**: A new Accessibility section in Settings under Interface lets you switch the interface and manuscript editor to OpenDyslexic, a typeface designed to reduce common reading errors.

::feather:: **Improvements**

- **Color Your Current Line**: The current-line highlight in Manuscript Mode can now be tinted one of twelve theme-matched colors or a custom shade, with adjustable intensity.
- **A More Welcoming First-Time Setup**: First-time setup now offers importing an existing .penpoint book or skipping straight to your library.
- **A More Communicative "Saved" Menu**: Penpoint now speaks up if a backup can't complete, instead of staying silent.
- **Smart Quotes in More Places**: Smart quotes now kick in consistently across story arcs, plot points, items, locations, and notes.
- **Demeanor is Back**: New books include a Demeanor trait for tracking personality, and you can pick more than one when it fits.

::bug:: **Bug Fixes**

- **Windows Smart App Control**: Every part of the app is now signed, so it launches normally on PCs that were blocking it.
- **Book Covers Surviving a Move Between Computers**: Cover art, portraits, and inline images now carry over correctly when a .penpoint file crosses from Windows to Mac or Linux.
- **Cut and Enter in Chapter & Book View**: Cutting text or pressing Enter across a scene divider now works correctly, and dividers stay put.
- **Apostrophes Now Work in Search**: Searching for a possessive like "Sarah's" now matches correctly in both the manuscript Find bar and the main search bar.
- **Trait Dropdown Fixes**: Archived subtraits are hidden from the picker, and the order matches your sort preference.
- **Demo Book Keeps Coming Back**: If you delete the Sherlock Holmes demo book, it now stays gone.
- **Line Counter Going Wrong While Typing**: The "Line N of M" counter in Manuscript Mode no longer drifts during long typing stretches.

Thank you for testing Penpoint staging! Report any issues before production release.
