Staging beta 8 of v1.3.6 is the "Restore Means Restore" build. It fixes books that got stuck on "Combining your changes," makes restoring a backup over a Cloud book mean what it says, and smooths opening and closing Penpoint on every computer. It also lands ProWritingAid and Grammarly suggestions in the right spot on Mac again, and Scrivener projects split into Acts or Parts now import as one whole book. Please try restoring a backup over a synced book, quitting and quickly reopening Penpoint, accepting a few writing assistant suggestions on a Mac, and importing a Scrivener project or a manuscript that has scene breaks.

::party-popper:: **New Features**

- **Scenes Come In With Your Manuscript**: Importing from Word, Scrivener, Dabble, or another app now brings your scenes in as separate scenes, split wherever you marked a scene break with #, * * *, or a divider line. The preview shows how many scenes you will get.
- **Justify Text**: Manuscript Mode's toolbar has a Justify button next to Left, Center, and Right. Justified paragraphs carry through to Word, EPUB, and Rich Text exports.
- **Delete From Minipen**: Characters, Items, and Locations in the Minipen now have a Delete button at the bottom of the page in edit mode, with the same confirmation as Worldbuilding.
- **Cloud Crash Course**: A few short slides explain how Penpoint Cloud works, what the two kinds of Saved mean, and why you can keep writing offline. Every Cloud writer sees it once per computer.
- **Era Change Heads-Up**: Changing or deleting an era in Calendar Settings now shows which of your existing dates will look different, so you can undo it before it takes effect.
- **Share My Writing Stats Follows You**: With Penpoint Cloud and "Sync my settings" on, turning Penpoint Global sharing on or off on one computer now applies to all of them.

::feather:: **Improvements**

- **Books Open on Your Newest Words**: Opening a book, or clicking back into Penpoint, right after writing on your other computer brings in your latest text within seconds. While Penpoint is still catching up other books, the one you open is fetched first.
- **Your Book Opens By Itself**: The "open on another computer" screen now opens your book on its own as soon as you close it there.
- **The Opening Screen Tells You What It Is Doing**: When a book needs your newer writing from another computer, the loading screen says "Bringing in changes from another device" instead of sitting on "Opening."
- **Calmer Retries When the Cloud Is Busy**: Writing while Penpoint Cloud is slow no longer makes Penpoint retry every few seconds, and a save that has to re-send a picture waits out a short slowdown instead of starting over.
- **Scene Versions Travel to Scrivener**: Your saved scene versions now export as Scrivener snapshots, so a round trip keeps your version history.
- **Multi-Act Scrivener Books Import Whole**: A Scrivener draft split into Acts or Parts comes in as one book, and when Penpoint asks, there is an "All of them" choice.
- **Story Arc Labels Stay Readable**: Plot point names on the Arcs page no longer pile on top of each other or get cut off.
- **Book Language Lives in Spellcheck**: The per-book writing language settings now sit inside Spellcheck and Autocorrect, behind a switch for the current book.

::bug:: **Bug Fixes**

- **Restoring a Backup Over a Cloud Book**: Restoring a backup or replacing your copy from a file now makes that version the book everywhere, on every device, instead of blending it with the Cloud copy or bringing back things you had deleted.
- **Books Stuck on "Combining Your Changes"**: If a book's changes can't be combined, Penpoint now sorts it out on its own within about an hour instead of trying forever. If the writing on both sides needs keeping, the other version is saved as a separate copy.
- **Closing Penpoint Always Frees Your Book**: Quitting with Cmd+Q, the Dock, or a shutdown now saves your last changes to the Cloud just like the close button does, so your book is not left marked as open on your other computer.
- **Two Devices, Same New Field, Book Keeps Syncing**: If you and another device both added the same thing at once (a custom character field, a dropdown option, an image in the same slot, the same word), Cloud sync no longer gets stuck on that book.
- **Big First Uploads Keep Going**: When Penpoint Cloud was busy, the first upload of a book with lots of pictures could stall and start over. Penpoint now keeps every picture that made it and retries only the rest.
- **No Conflict Copy From a Cloud Blip**: A brief Cloud hiccup during a sync check no longer leaves a "kept from your other device" copy of scenes you never edited elsewhere.
- **Catching Up After Your Other Computer Saved**: When your other computer saved while this one was catching up, Penpoint merges the two normally instead of keeping a separate copy of your work.
- **Move, Split, and Delete Without Signing In**: A book that came from Penpoint Cloud can now be moved into a series, split out of one, or deleted on a computer that has not signed in yet. Cloud catches up the next time you sign in.
- **Importing a Book You Deleted**: Importing a copy of a book you deleted everywhere brings it back as a new book, whether or not you are signed in yet.
- **Series Names Follow the Same-Name Rule**: Converting a book into a series or renaming a series now tells you when another book or series already has that name. Splitting a series numbers a new book that would share a name, like "Dune #2."
- **Shelf Repair Keeps Your New Book**: When Penpoint repairs a damaged book list, a book you create at the same moment can no longer drop off the shelf until the next launch.
- **Index Cards Stay Put When a Chapter Is Deleted**: Deleting or merging away a chapter leaves the index cards that sat after it where they were.
- **Mac Paragraph Spacing Matches Your Setting Again**: On macOS, the gap between paragraphs is back to exactly what you chose in Settings, matching Windows.
- **Suggestions Land Where They Should**: On macOS, accepting a ProWritingAid or Grammarly suggestion no longer lands one character off below chapter headings, headings, lists, and quotes.
- **Window Opening**: On a Mac, Penpoint's window no longer flashes and jumps around as it opens, and on Linux it now remembers your window size and position.
- **Character List Keeps Up With Edits**: Renaming a character or changing their nicknames shows in the character list right away.
- **Carried-Over Calendar Dates**: When a chapter's Calendar opens with your last-used date filled in, leaving edit mode keeps that date instead of reverting to "No date set." Clicking past a chapter without finishing does not save it.
- **EPUB Re-Import**: EPUB files exported from Penpoint no longer come back as empty chapters when imported again.
- **Add to Dictionary No Longer Ignores Capitals**: Adding a word you had already added with different capitalization now works instead of doing nothing.

Thank you for testing Penpoint staging! Report any issues before production release.
