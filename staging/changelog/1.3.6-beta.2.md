This is staging beta 2 of v1.3.6. Since beta 1, the focus has been on making Sync quieter and smarter: books that were edited on two computers now merge themselves in the background, more of your settings follow you between machines, and Cloud billing and account recovery got friendlier. Please put multi-computer editing through its paces.

::party-popper:: **New Features**

- **Manage Your Cloud Subscription in Penpoint**: Switch between monthly and yearly, cancel, or resume, all from Settings under Penpoint Cloud. Switching to yearly shows about how much you'll pay today before you confirm.

::bug:: **Bug Fixes**

- **Books Edited on Two Computers Merge on Their Own**: No more amber warning waiting on you to open the book. Penpoint merges the two versions in the background, and the rare cases that used to leave a book stuck now sort themselves out.
- **Sync Sorting Itself Out**: Fixed a false "Sync needs attention" warning on books that were already safely backed up. Sync now retries and recovers on its own in the background, with no Manual Cloud Backup needed.
- **Manuscript Settings Now Sync Across Computers**: Four Manuscript view settings stayed on one computer instead of syncing: outline word counts, the confirm prompts for merging scenes and deleting comments, and hiding the comments column. They now follow you like your other preferences.
- **A Confusing Plan-Change Screen**: Switching Cloud plans used to hand you off to a billing page with misleading wording, then later a dead end. Plan changes now happen inside Penpoint, with a clear confirmation and honest wording about what you pay and when.
- **Signing In on a Computer Penpoint Cloud Didn't Know Yet**: A computer with an older sign-in could get stuck bouncing between the password prompt and the sign-in screen. It now sends you a fresh code to connect that computer first, and every Cloud screen has a Sign Out link so you can always start over.
- **A Signed-Out Account Letting Go Cleanly**: Signing out and back in as a different account no longer leaves the previous account's lease running for an open book. The old session releases its hold right away. Your local writing is never touched.
- **A Revoked Computer Clearing Its Keys Right Away**: When a computer's Cloud access is revoked, the sign-in flow now clears that computer's cached keys the moment it reports the revocation, and finishes the cleanup on the next launch even if it was interrupted partway. Your writing on that computer is never touched.

Thank you for testing Penpoint staging! Report any issues before production release.
