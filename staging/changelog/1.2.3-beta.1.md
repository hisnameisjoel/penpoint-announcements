This is the first staging beta of v1.2.3, bringing tables to every rich-text field in Penpoint, a new way to pin your key characters to the top of your sidebar, and a batch of polish and fixes across the app.

::party-popper:: **New Features**

- **Tables Everywhere**: Insert a table into any rich text area, including notes, character and item descriptions, location pages, chapter author notes, and arc details. Click the grid icon in the toolbar, drag to pick your size (up to 6 columns by 20 rows), and click Insert Table. Each table gets a caption, and you can right-click any cell to add a row or column. Bold, italic, bullet lists, and @mentions all work inside cells. One caveat: tables export as a named placeholder and appendix in Word, PDF, and other formats.
- **Pin Characters to the Top**: Right-click any character and choose "Pin to top" to lock your protagonist or key characters into a permanent Pinned section at the top of the sidebar. Pinned characters stay put no matter how you sort the rest of the list. Right-click again to unpin.
- **Horizontal Lines in Notes and Descriptions**: Insert a horizontal divider into any rich-text field using the new line icon in the toolbar. Lines export cleanly to Word, PDF, RTF, and Markdown.

::feather:: **Improvements**

- **Same-Day Events on Calendar Blocks**: When a scene starts and ends on the same day, the Calendar block now shows it as a single tidy entry instead of two stacked date blocks.
- **Calendar Picker Layout**: The month and year are now centered between the navigation arrows in the calendar picker, instead of sitting off to one side with empty space.

::bug:: **Bug Fixes**

- **Create New Tag Polish**: Pressing Enter no longer overrides your color choice, and the auto-suggested color now picks an unused one from your palette instead of always defaulting to the first.
- **Quick Create (Ctrl+N) Polish**: Nicknames you typed for a new character were being silently discarded. Dropdowns like Importance and Type are now visibly highlighted when navigating with arrow keys. Item type and chapter notes labels are now consistent throughout the app.
- **Minipen Width When Switching Monitors**: Moving Penpoint to a monitor with a different size or resolution could shrink the Minipen sidebar too narrow, causing manuscript text to scroll sideways. It now snaps to the correct minimum width.
- **Row Highlight After Pressing End**: On a wrapped paragraph, pressing End dropped the highlight band to the next line even though the cursor stayed put. The band now follows the cursor.
- **Minipen Scene Selector Font**: The scene list in the Minipen Chapters tab was rendering in a smaller font than the surrounding panel. It now matches.
- **Text Selection in Outline Blocks**: You can now select and copy text from Outline blocks while using Minipen.
- **Image Block: Minipen Display and Lightbox Captions**: Images set to "Contain" were being cropped into a small box in the Minipen sidebar. They now show at their natural proportions. The image lightbox also now respects your caption position preference.
- **Chapter Tags Now Visible in Chapter View**: You can now assign tags directly to a chapter. The Tags block shows a "Whole chapter" section at the top, with scene tags listed below. The section stays hidden until you enter edit mode if no chapter tags are set.
- **Confirmation Dialogs Now Wait for Your Answer**: Clicking the trash icon on an image block could delete the image before the confirmation popup appeared. All confirmation prompts now properly wait for your response.
- **Chapters Tutorial**: Fixed the Chapters page tutorial failing to start and run properly in some cases.
- **Story Arcs in Minipen**: The arc visualizer was getting squished in the narrow Minipen sidebar. It is now hidden there, leaving the Whole Chapter and Scenes lists to handle the view.
- **Item Names No Longer Get Clipped in Cards**: Items with longer names could get cut off mid-word in the Items grid. Names now wrap and hyphenate properly.

Thank you for testing Penpoint staging! Report any issues before production release.
