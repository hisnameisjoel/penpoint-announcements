Staging beta 9 of v1.3.6 is mostly Scrivener and Markdown import and export fixes, plus a few smaller ones.

::feather:: **Improvements**

- **Scrivener Headings Name Your Chapter**: A chapter that opens with heading lines you marked in Scrivener now takes its name from them.
- **Mark Your Own Previous Versions**: On the "Point to Your Folders" step of a Scrivener import, you can mark any folder or document as a previous version.
- **Same Plus for New Chapters**: Adding a chapter from the Chapters page now puts it after the chapter you have selected, like Manuscript does.
- **Delete Dialog Explains Repairs**: Deleting a book Penpoint is still repairing now says so, and that signing in to Penpoint Cloud lets the repair finish.

::bug:: **Bug Fixes**

- **Acts Import as Parts**: Scrivener Act folders now import as parts holding their own chapters, not one giant chapter.
- **Smarter Scrivener Guesses**: Scene counts match your book, planning folders like Outlines no longer become Characters, and short scenes are no longer filed as old drafts.
- **Exports Use Your Chapter Numbers**: Markdown and Scrivener exports use the chapter numbers you see in Penpoint, label chapters from your other Books, and name files like "Ch05 Death on the Moor."
- **Manuscript Stays Put**: Closing the "..." menu, zoom menu, or highlight picker no longer scrolls your manuscript up.
- **Tutorial Waits Its Turn**: The Manuscript tutorial no longer starts on top of the "How would you like to get started?" window.
- **Uploads Survive a Long Pause**: A Cloud upload interrupted for about two hours now picks up instead of stopping on a hold.

Thank you for testing Penpoint staging! Report any issues before production release.
